About this world

We build and explore together. Boss fights are group events planned in Discord.

Server rules

  • Play fair: no cheats, exploits, or griefing.
  • Respect other players and their belongings. Ask before taking anything; we are happy to help if you need supplies.
  • You may bring existing characters and equipment, but do not server-hop to move items or bypass progression.
  • Boss fights are group events. We aim to take on about one boss per week; check Discord to arrange the next fight.
  • Mark cleared dungeons with a wooden cross at the entrance so others know.
  • Use your in-game name, or a recognizable version of it, in Discord.
  • Keep the “Visible to other players” setting enabled.

These rules rely on trust, respect, and common sense. Have fun and skål!

Server rules
